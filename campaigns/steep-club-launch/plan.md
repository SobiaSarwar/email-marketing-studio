# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

Draft for owner review. Test content only. Nothing here has been sent.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Your First Cup
- **Summary:** A four-week email series that invites our waitlist to start The Steep Club, our monthly tea box.
- **Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription within the first month.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups signs up most.
  - Collect questions and objections to use in later emails.
  - Keep unsubscribes low.
- **Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. (First Monday of next month is 7 December.)

**A note on the goal.** 300 of about 2,000 is a 15% sign-up rate. Waitlists often convert well, but this is a high bar. We will track it weekly. We will also watch for a smaller result, such as 150 to 200, so we are not surprised.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 people on our waitlist. All of them asked to hear from us. They know the name already.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | New to loose-leaf tea | An easy, friendly start | "I don't know what to pick" or "I'll do it wrong" |
| Gift buyers | Buying for someone else | A thoughtful gift that is simple to give | Unsure what is in it, or when it will arrive |
| Daily tea drinkers | Already drink tea most days | New teas and small-batch finds | "Will this be better than what I buy now?" |

**Buying stage:** Consideration to decision. They have already shown interest.

**How we split them:** Use the group tag on each lead in `leads/`. If a lead has no tag, they get the general version.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** A small box of loose-leaf tea, every month, for your quiet moments.

Supporting messages:

1. **Beginners:** You do not need to know anything. We tell you how to steep each tea.
2. **Gift buyers:** A tea box is a gift that keeps going. We will say clearly what is in the box.
3. **Daily drinkers:** Small-batch, loose-leaf teas to try beyond your usual cup.
4. **Everyone:** We are honest about what is in the box and what is not.

**Proof points.** We only use what the owner confirms. Do not invent any.
- The exact teas and items in the first box.
- Where the teas come from, if the owner provides it.
- Real quotes from waitlist members, with their permission.

**Voice:** Warm, cozy, honest. Follows `brand/voice.md`.

## 4. Channel strategy

Email only. No paid ads, as agreed.

| Channel | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Email to the waitlist | They asked to hear from us. Cheapest way to reach them. | Medium |
| Sign-up page on our site | Where every email sends people. Must be ready before the first send. | Medium |
| Reply and question handling | Quick, kind answers help people decide. | Low |

Not in this plan: social posts, influencers and ads. Any can be added later by the owner.

## 7. Success metrics

**Primary:** 300 new subscriptions from the waitlist by 3 January 2027.

**Secondary:**
- Click rate on each email.
- Sign-up page visit to sign-up rate.
- Sign-ups by group (beginner, gift, daily).
- Unsubscribe rate per email.
- Replies and questions received.

**How we track:** Count sign-ups from the shop or subscription tool. Read email stats from the email tool. Log it all in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/` each week.

**Weekly check-in:** Every Monday. Look at last week's numbers and adjust the next emails.

## 8. Budget

Small, email only. No ad spend.

- **Main cost:** team time to write, test and answer replies.
- **Tools:** whatever email and shop tools we already use.
- **Owner to confirm:** any spend on product photos, sample teas or tool upgrades. No dollar amounts are set in this plan.
- **Contingency:** keep a small reserve for a tool upgrade or a missed item.

## 9. Risks and what we do about them

1. **15% sign-up rate may be too high.** Set a "good" level and a "great" level with the owner. Review at the end of week 2.
2. **Holiday timing.** Gift buyers may worry about timing. We make no delivery date promises. The sign-up page must state what the owner can honestly confirm.
3. **Too many emails.** Seven emails in four weeks could tire people. Buyers stop getting them. Watch unsubscribes. Cut Email 6 if the rate climbs.
4. **Unclear offer.** If price, contents or offer are missing, emails will feel vague. The owner must provide them before drafting.
