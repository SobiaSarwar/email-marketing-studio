# Kettlebird Tea Sampler Box: Customer Reviews (1 of 6)

Practice data. All reviews are made up.

### 1. Priya M. - 5/5
The Darjeeling was the star for me. Light, floral and lovely with a slice of toast. Will order again.

### 2. Tom H. - 3/5
Tea was good, but the box arrived dented and one pouch had split. Found loose Sencha at the bottom.

### 3. Ayesha K. - 4/5
Great mix. The Masala Chai was spicy and warming. Wish the sachets were bigger, I only got two cups.

### 4. Greg L. - 2/5
Took 11 days to arrive. The tea is fine but I bought it as a birthday gift and it was late.

### 5. Maria S. - 5/5
Jasmine Pearls opened up so prettily in my glass mug. My mum loved them too.

### 6. Daniel O. - 3/5

### 7. Hannah B. - 4/5
Earl Grey was fragrant without being soapy. Peppermint was a bit weak, though.

### 8. Luis R. - 5/5
Genmaicha tastes like toasted rice and a cozy evening. Already finished it.

### 9. Chloe W. - 1/5
Box was crushed and two pouches were empty. Emailed twice and had no reply for a week.

### 10. Ben T. - 4/5
Good value for trying new teas. The Lapsang Souchong was too smoky for me, but my dad loved it.

### 11. Fatima A. - 5/5
Rooibos Vanilla is now my bedtime cup. The box was neat and the little tins were a nice touch.

### 12. Oliver P. - 3/5

### 13. Sophie D. - 4/5

### 14. Raj N. - 2/5

### 15. Emma J. - 5/5
Hojicha, roasty and smooth. I'd buy a full bag in a heartbeat.

### 16. Carlos V. - 3/5

### 17. Nina F. - 4/5
Lemon Ginger was bright and zingy. The Yunnan Gold was rich and a little sweet.

### 18. Sam C. - 5/5
Bought it as a gift for my sister. She texted me a photo of her first cup. Very happy.

### 19. Dana R. - 2/5

### 20. Will G. - 4/5
