# Handoff: email 01 subject angles
- Tea-curious beginners want an easy, friendly start with no tea knowledge needed.
- Gift buyers want a thoughtful idea for someone else's quiet moments.
- Daily drinkers want something that fits the cup they already have each day.
- Angle A (calm daily ritual): "Make room for a quiet cup", "A small ritual, steeped for you", "The Steep Club is here: your daily cup".
- Angle B (discovering new teas): "Find a new tea to sip", "Small-batch teas, ready to try", "Your next favorite cup starts here".
- My pick: Angle A. It fits all three groups, and our voice is cozy and calm.
- Angle B leans on the teas, and we can't say what is in the box until the owner confirms.
- Best single line: "The Steep Club is here: your daily cup". It keeps the current subject and stays under 50 characters.
- Open item: the owner must confirm "small-batch" before we use Angle B option 2.
