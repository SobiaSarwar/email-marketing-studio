# Angle B: Discovering new teas

Three subject lines for email 01 (The Steep Club is here). Each one is 50 characters or fewer.

1. **Find a new tea to sip** (23 characters)
2. **Small-batch teas, ready to try** (32 characters)
3. **Your next favorite cup starts here** (36 characters)

## Notes

- All three use words from our voice: sip, small-batch, cup.
- None says what is in the box. What comes in each box still needs the owner to confirm.
- None makes a health claim, a delivery promise, a price claim or a "best" claim.
- Option 2 mentions small-batch teas. Check with the owner that this is true of the Club's teas before using it.
- Preview text can stay as it is: "You joined the waitlist. Now you can start your box."
