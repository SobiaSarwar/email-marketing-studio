# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` - brand voice, logo notes, product details and anything that defines how the business sounds.
- `leads/` - contact lists and audience notes (use made-up or test data only).
- `campaigns/` - campaign plans, email sequences and finished email drafts.
- `notes/` - research, decisions and working notes, such as which skills we chose.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Keep sentences short and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Drafts and test content only.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
- Save every email draft in `campaigns/<campaign-name>/emails/`. The email rules only load for files in an `emails/` folder.
