# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, for people on our waitlist. We write to three groups: tea-curious beginners, gift buyers and daily tea drinkers.

## Our voice in three words

**Warm. Cozy. Honest.**

- Warm: talk like a friendly shopkeeper. Use "you" and "we".
- Cozy: small, calm moments. A cup, a quiet corner, a rainy afternoon.
- Honest: say what is in the box and what is not. No hype.

Keep sentences short and plain.

## Words we use

steep, cup, ritual, small-batch, cozy, loose-leaf, sip, "your box"

## Words we avoid

- Hype: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "premium"
- Pressure: "last chance", "hurry", "don't miss out"
- Health buzzwords: "guilt-free", "superfood", "detox"
- Jargon: "synergy", "leverage"

## Claims we must never make

- No health or medical claims. Never say tea detoxes, cures, boosts immunity, helps weight loss or eases anxiety.
- Say "caffeine-free" only for teas where it is true, and only when the product page confirms it.
- No promises about delivery dates.
- No made-up prices or discounts. Prices and offers come from the owner.
- No "best" or "number one" claims.
- No fake scarcity, such as "only a few boxes left".
- No promises the team can't keep, such as "we read every reply".

## How we sign off

End every email with:

Warmly,
The Kettlebird Tea team
